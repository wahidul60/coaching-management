# Functional Requirements & Business Workflow Analysis: Coaching Management Software

## 1. Project Overview

The Coaching Management Software is designed to automate and secure the administrative workflows of a coaching institution, specifically focusing on:
- Student registration and profile management.
- Course creation and fee configuration by administrators.
- Course enrollment and payment entry by receptionists.
- Secure payment verification and approval/rejection by founding members.
- Printable payment card generation with unique QR codes for instant verification.

Technology Stack:
- **Frontend**: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM
- **Architecture**: Feature-based Modular Monolith with REST API

---

## 2. Business Workflow

The end-to-end operational lifecycle follows six sequential steps:

1. **Helpdesk Registration**: A student arrives at the Helpdesk. The Helpdesk searches by Token Number or identifying information. If existing, the profile is verified. If new, a profile is created and a unique Token Number is assigned.
2. **Receptionist Course Selection**: The student proceeds to the Reception Desk with their Token Number. The receptionist retrieves the student profile, selects an available ADMIN-managed course, views the course fee, enters the amount received and payment details, and submits the payment entry for approval.
3. **Payment Entry**: The system records the transaction with status `PENDING`, assigns a unique payment reference, and notifies the Founding Member dashboard. No payment card is generated at this stage.
4. **Founding Member Verification**: Founding Members review pending payments on their dashboard, cross-referencing student profile, course fee, and amount received. They either approve the payment or reject it with a mandatory reason.
5. **Payment Card Generation**: Upon successful approval, the system generates a print-friendly Payment Card containing institution branding, student details, course info, payment status, watermark, and a secure QR code.
6. **QR Code Verification**: Scanning the QR code on the payment card opens a secure verification portal that validates the payment status and displays valid student/course information without exposing sensitive private contact data.

## 3. Helpdesk Requirements

- **Student Search**: Ability to search existing student records instantly using Token Number, name, or phone number.
- **Profile Verification**: Display existing student information to confirm identity before forwarding to reception.
- **New Profile Creation**: Form to capture mandatory student particulars for unregistered attendees.

---

## 4. Student Registration Requirements

Mandatory data fields for registering a new student profile:
- Full Name
- Roll Number
- Student Phone (Self)
- Guardian Phone
- Blood Group
- School/College (Short Name)
- Academic Batch
- Student Photo (Digital upload)
- **Token Number**: Unique identifier generated by the system upon successful registration, serving as the primary anchor across all downstream modules.

---

## 5. Receptionist Requirements

- **Token Lookup**: Search and select student profiles using their unique Token Number.
- **Profile Display**: View verified student details.
- **Course Selection UI**: Browse and select from active, ADMIN-configured courses categorized under Academic, Admission, or Other.
- **Payment Entry Form**: Input received amount, remaining balance calculation, payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Mobile Wallet, etc.), transaction reference, payment date, and optional remarks.
- **Submission**: Submit completed payment entry to the approval queue with status `PENDING`.

---

## 6. Course Selection Requirements

- **Admin Management**: Courses and their baseline fees are created, updated, and managed exclusively by ADMIN users.
- **Categories**: Support for Academic Courses, Admission Courses, and Other Courses.
- **Fee Integrity**: Course fees are read-only for Receptionists; zero fee tampering or overrides are permitted at the reception desk.


---

## 7. Payment Management Requirements

- **Initial State**: All newly submitted payments must be created with status `PENDING`.
- **Payment Reference**: Unique reference identifier generated for every transaction attempt.
- **Audit Trails**: Recording of Receptionist ID, timestamp, transaction details, and payment method.
- **Immutability**: Approved payments cannot be silently modified or tampered with.

---

## 8. Founding Member Approval Requirements

- **Dedicated Dashboard**: Real-time view listing all pending payment approval requests.
- **Comprehensive View**: Side-by-side or tabbed display of Student Information, Course Information, and Payment Information.
- **Approval Action**: Secure authorization action that transitions payment status from `PENDING` to `APPROVED`, recording the Founding Member's ID and approval timestamp.
- **Rejection Action**: Action to reject a payment with a **mandatory rejection reason**, transitioning the payment status to `REJECTED` and recording the reviewer's ID and timestamp.

---

## 9. Payment Card Requirements

- **Conditional Generation**: Triggered automatically *only* upon successful payment approval by a Founding Member.
- **Design & Layout**: Print-friendly layout suitable for physical printing or digital download.
- **Required Elements**:
  - Coaching Institution Logo
  - Student Photo
  - Student Name & Token Number
  - Student Roll & School/College Short Name
  - Academic Batch
  - Course Name
  - Payment Details (Amount paid, status)
  - Unique QR Code
  - Institution Watermark

---

## 10. QR Verification Requirements

- **Public Verification Endpoint**: Accessible via QR code scan without requiring full admin authentication.
- **Backend Validation**: Validates the cryptographic or unique token embedded in the QR code against the database.
- **Display Output**: Shows approval validity status, student name, roll, batch, and course name.
- **Privacy Protection**: Strictly hides sensitive private information (such as student phone numbers and guardian contact details) from public view during QR scans.

## 12. Business Rules

1. **Unique Token Number**: Every registered student must be assigned a globally unique Token Number.
2. **Searchability**: Token Numbers must be indexed and instantly searchable by Helpdesk and Receptionists.
3. **Duplicate Prevention**: Existing students must be verified and reused rather than re-registered.
4. **Admin Course Governance**: Only ADMIN users can create, edit, or configure course fees.
5. **Fee Immutability**: Receptionists cannot modify course fees configured by ADMIN.
6. **Pending Default**: All newly submitted payment records must start in `PENDING` status.
7. **Authorized Approval**: Only users with the `FOUNDING_MEMBER` role can approve or reject payments.
8. **Mandatory Rejection Reason**: Rejecting a payment requires providing and storing a text reason.
9. **Conditional Card Issuance**: Payment cards can only be generated for *approved* payments.
10. **Audit Logging**: Every approval, rejection, and payment entry must record actor IDs and timestamps.
11. **Payment Immutability**: Approved payments are immutable and cannot be silently altered.
12. **Secure QR Verification**: QR verification endpoints must validate tokens securely while protecting student privacy.

## 13. Confirmed Requirements

- Four distinct user roles (`ADMIN`, `HELP_DESK`, `RECEPTIONIST`, `FOUNDING_MEMBER`).
- Token-based student identification workflow.
- Mandatory Founding Member approval for all payments before card generation.
- Secure QR verification hiding sensitive contact info.
- Modular monolith backend architecture with REST APIs.

---

## 14. Recommended Business Rules

1. **Token Format**: Standardize token generation format (e.g., prefix + year + sequential number, such as `CMS-2026-0001`) to ensure readability and uniqueness.
2. **Token Permanence**: Token numbers should remain permanent across multiple academic sessions or course enrollments for the same student.
3. **Rejection Resubmission**: If a payment is rejected, the receptionist should be able to correct the entry and resubmit it as a new pending transaction linked to the original reference.
4. **Audit Trails**: Maintain a comprehensive immutable audit log table (`payment_audit_logs`) tracking all state changes from `PENDING` to `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`.

---

## 15. Unresolved Questions

1. **Token Generation Mechanism**: Should Token Numbers be auto-incremented integers, randomized alphanumeric codes, or structured semantic strings (e.g., branch + year + sequence)?
2. **Multi-Course Enrollment**: Can a student enroll in multiple courses under a single registration token, and does each course require a separate payment entry and payment card?
3. **Partial Payments**: Does the system support partial payments (installments) for a single course, or must the full course fee be paid in one transaction?
4. **Multiple Payments**: Can a student make multiple payment submissions for the same course if an initial payment was rejected or split?
5. **Admin Override**: Can an `ADMIN` override a Founding Member's rejection or approval in exceptional circumstances?
6. **Refund Management**: Who possesses the authority to process refunds, and how are refunded payment cards invalidated?
7. **Outstanding Balances**: What specific operational restrictions apply when a student has an outstanding balance on a course fee?

---

## 16. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-01 (Helpdesk Registration)**: Helpdesk users can successfully search students by Token Number and create new student profiles with all mandatory fields and auto-assigned Token Numbers.
- **AC-02 (Reception Course Selection)**: Receptionists can retrieve student profiles by Token Number, select ADMIN-managed courses, input payment amounts without editing course fees, and submit payment records.
- **AC-03 (Payment Pending State)**: Submitted payments are successfully stored with `PENDING` status and appear on Founding Member dashboards without generating payment cards.
- **AC-04 (Founding Member Verification)**: Founding Members can review complete student, course, and payment details, and successfully approve or reject payments (with mandatory reason on rejection).
- **AC-05 (Card Generation)**: Payment cards are automatically generated only for approved payments, incorporating institution branding, student photo, details, and a secure QR code.
- **AC-06 (Secure QR Verification)**: Scanning an approved payment card's QR code securely validates the payment and displays non-sensitive student/course details while withholding private phone numbers.
- **AC-07 (Authorization Enforcement)**: Backend REST API endpoints correctly enforce role-based access control (RBAC), preventing unauthorized role actions (e.g., Receptionists approving payments or changing course fees).
